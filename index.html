🍪 Cookie and Privacy Policy

Benefits: Cookies help us provide you with a faster and more personalized shopping experience. For example, your cart items are not lost, and your language and theme preferences are remembered. It also helps us offer you special discount coupons and campaigns instantly.

Risks: Collecting cookies may lead to the analysis of your browsing habits. In order to present personalized ads, this data may be shared with our advertising and analytics partners (third-party service providers) in accordance with privacy agreements.

Legal Information: The collected data is processed only for the specified purposes in accordance with data protection laws (e.g. GDPR). You can clear cookies from your browser settings at any time.

📋 Data Collected

  • Cookies: Site preferences, session info, analytical data
  • Device info: Browser type, screen resolution, language preference
  • Interaction data: Coupon usage and campaign preferences

By clicking the "I Accept" button, you confirm that you have read and accepted all the policies above. For more information, please visit our Privacy Notice.

Location Permission Required

You need to allow location access to get your coupon code. If your browser blocked the location request, please follow these steps:

  1. Go to Settings from your browser's menu (⋮).
  2. Click on Privacy and security → Site settings.
  3. Enter the Location permissions section.
  4. Change the location permission for our site to Allow.
  5. Click the Try Again button below.

Shortcut: You can also enable location by clicking the Lock (🔒) icon in the address bar.
